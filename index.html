Local Author’s Tale “The Price of the Wind” Accepted by Flash Fiction Magazine

By Our Literary Correspondent

In a development that surprised no one, A.T. Lanthier has received a publication for the story “The Price of the Wind” in Flash Fiction Magazine, the esteemed online publication that publishes in print once a year.

While the piece is political in nature, some hail the author for mulling many different perspectives in few words.

A Long Way to a Thousand Words

From afar, A.T. Lanthier is known for sitting in cafes, drinking coffee too late and keeping private with his notebook. Asked about the work, the author was characteristically candid: “I write about everything, whatever comes to mind with no limits. Songs, future conversations, I wrote a novel once. It’s terrible.”

Aside from political fiction, he takes pleasure in writing urban fiction and dabbles in the fantasy genres as well.

“I write about everything, whatever comes to mind with no limits. Songs, future conversations, I wrote a novel once. It’s terrible.”

Readers eager to see the finished work are advised to keep watch on the magazine’s pages in the coming weeks. Further details, including the date of publication, will be announced in these columns as soon as they may be printed.

In the meantime, the author has returned to his typewriter to edit some of his rejected stories. Those wishing to follow along may find news of them elsewhere in this edition.

About the Author

A.T. Lanthier writes fiction long and short of the new and before. When not at the typewriter, the author may be found reading the next Kazuro Ishiguro or Barbara Kingsolver novel.
